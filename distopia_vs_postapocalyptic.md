Blade Runner is a dystopian sci-fi film that doesn’t expand into the book’s references to World War Terminus and the ecological collapse caused by radioactive fallout that killed most animal species, giving rise to replicant pets.  The only evident ecological decline in Blade Runner is represented by a near constant drizzle and lack of real animals.  By contrast, Blade Runner 2049 is far more post-apocalyptic than the original, as it shows Soylent Green-evoking overcrowding (which is odd, considering population COLLAPSE is portrayed and described in the original), extensive deserts, rising sea levels, violent wasteland scavengers, and snow in LA. 

That got me thinking about both sub-genres, so I decided to make a list of every post-apocalyptic and dystopian film I could find through multiple sources based on the following criteria, while trying to avoid sub-genre crossovers and keeping the two categories “pure.”

For post apocalyptic:

• nuclear war
• pandemic
• ecological collapse
• unspecified civilizational collapse

No aliens, space travel, time travel, monsters, zombies, or AI takeovers (sorry, 12 Monkeys, Terminator, I Am Legend, and Interstellar fans!)

For dystopian:

• AI/androids/robots
• authoritarian/police states.

No space travel, time travel, aliens, genetically-modified or otherwise sentient animals (sorry, Planet of the Apes fans!)

Now that I’m happy with both lists, I thought I’d share them with my fellow fans. If you feel I missed any, bitched the criteria filters, or you take issue with any film on either list, please comment and elaborate your reasons - these lists are far from authoritative! 🙂  And away we go!
